# My 6 Months in Data

A personal data visualisation project where, for six months, I will collect daily data about my life and use it to create a visual representation of the period.

Inspired by personal data projects shared on [r/dataisbeautiful](https://www.reddit.com/r/dataisbeautiful/), the aim is to turn six months of everyday experiences into a single data-driven story.

The data can be found on this publicly viewable Google Sheet [https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1TlGUFFiFzKQdvGUBoW0lOn6G6Zs7lUzghnvx7nDMHPQ/edit?usp=sharing], with my R code and poster UI visible within the R and Output folders of this repository.
## Project

**Start date:** 16 November 2026  
**Duration:** 6 months  
**End date:** 16 May 2027  
**Data collection:** Daily  
**Final output:** Portrait A4-width, unlimited-height 2D poster

The final visualisation will combine the 6 months of daily observations into one long-form poster, allowing patterns, changes and relationships across the period to emerge.

## Daily Measures

Each day I will record the following measures:

| # | Measure | Type |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | How happy I felt | 0–10 |
| 2 | Things I was grateful for | Qualitative |
| 3 | Time I got out of bed | Time |
| 4 | Time I put my phone away | Time |
| 5 | Number of people I spoke to | Category |
| 6 | Whether I did coaching | Yes/No |
| 7 | Whether I did university work | Yes/No |
| 8 | Where I spent most of the day | Category |
| 9 | Number of coffees | Count |
| 10 | Whether I cried | Yes/No |
| 11 | Whether I was ill | Yes/No |
| 12 | Whether the day felt good | Yes/No |
| 13 | Why the day felt good, or why it did not | Qualitative |
| 14 | Top 3 artists of the day | Ranked |
| 15 | Top 3 songs of the day | Ranked |
| 16 | Whether I left the house | Yes/No |
| 17 | Whether I went on a walk | Yes/No |
| 18 | Whether I think I got at least 1% better | Yes/No |

## Data Collection

Data will be collected once per day, ideally at the end of the day between **7–9pm**.

The goal is to keep the process quick enough that it remains sustainable for the entire six months.

## Final Visualisation

The final output will be designed as a:

- **Portrait A4-width** poster
- **Unlimited height** / long-form 2D layout
- Six-month timeline running through the visualisation
- Combination of quantitative and qualitative data
- Visual patterns showing changes over time
- Potential comparisons between different aspects of daily life

The final design will be created only after the full six-month dataset has been collected, rather than deciding in advance exactly what the visualisation will look like.

## Questions I Want the Data to Answer

By the end of the project, I want to be able to explore questions such as:

- When was I happiest?
- What does a "good day" actually look like?
- Does happiness relate to sleep/wake times?
- Does phone use relate to how I feel?
- How does social interaction vary across the six months?
- Do I feel better on days when I leave the house?
- Does walking relate to happiness?
- How does university work affect my day?
- How much does coaching feature in my routine?
- How does my coffee consumption change over time?
- Are there periods where I was noticeably more or less well?
- What music characterised different periods of my life?
- Are there recurring patterns in where I spend my time?
- What were the most significant periods of the six months?

## Goal

The objective isn't to optimise my life or produce a scientifically rigorous study.

It is to create a **data-driven record of six months of ordinary life** and see what patterns become visible when everyday experiences are turned into data.

At the end of the six months, I should have a dataset that would otherwise never exist — a quantitative record of what these six months actually looked like.
